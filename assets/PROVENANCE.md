## Living café 1.2.0 — 2026-09-05

City cats, dog, birds, expanded walkers/traffic, acoustic panels, stage, speakers and police/musician representations are original procedural Three.js geometry in `src/view/city.ts` and `src/view/scene.ts`. No new third-party model downloads. Live instrumental accompaniment and outdoor bird/traffic layers are generated by original Web Audio synthesis in `src/platform/audio.ts`; playlist mode reuses the previously documented licensed OGG. No commercial song recording or external streaming service is used.
